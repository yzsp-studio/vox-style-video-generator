## 🇨🇳 中文

把主题、文章或报告做成一支完整的 Vox 风格解释视频：先确认需求与制作方案，再出主视觉与关键帧、逐关键帧动效方案，最后完成中等偏强强度的动画、旁白、BGM、合成与质检。默认走纸张拼贴（Paper Collage）方向——编辑部证据板式的地图、照片、标签、箭头、卡片、半色调、胶带、图钉与印章；只有用户明确要求时才切角色剪纸（Cutout Character）方向。分镜里每个节拍都要写明主物理动作、辅助响应动效、镜头运动、声音提示与转场，并严格遵守物品有逻辑地进入、接触、固定、锁定的规则。方案未确认前不生成预览图、主视觉、动画、旁白或音乐，避免直接烧高成本媒体生成。适合科普、纪录短片与教育视频论文；不适合虚构长片，也不支持跳过动效方案确认。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 主题/文章/报告 → 完整解释视频（含完整旁白稿与逐节拍分镜表）
- 两套视觉方向：纸张拼贴（默认）与角色剪纸
- 主视觉与关键帧先审后做，确认才进动画
- 逐关键帧动效方案：主物理动作 + 辅助响应动效 + 转场
- 旁白、BGM、合成与质检一条龙，画幅与时长必须显式确认
- 物理连续性规则：物品有逻辑地进入、接触、固定、锁定

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a topic, article or report into a complete Vox-style explainer: confirm requirements, produce a production plan, then key visuals and keyframes, then a per-keyframe motion plan, and finally moderate-plus animation, narration, music, compositing and QC. The default direction is Paper Collage — an editorial evidence board of maps, photos, labels, arrows, cards, halftones, tape, pins and stamps — switching to Cutout Character paper puppets only when the user explicitly asks. Every beat in the shot list states its main physical action, secondary responsive motion, camera move, sound cue and transition, under a strict rule that objects enter, contact, settle and lock logically. Nothing is generated — no preview, key visual, keyframe, animation, narration or music — before the plan is approved, so no budget burns on unconfirmed media. Built for science explainers, documentary shorts and educational video essays; not for fiction features, and it will not skip motion-plan approval.

### ✨ What it does

- Topic, article or report into a finished explainer, with a full narration script and per-beat shot table
- Two visual directions: Paper Collage by default, Cutout Character on request
- Key visuals and keyframes reviewed and approved before any animation
- Per-keyframe motion plan: main physical action, secondary responsive motion, transitions
- Narration, BGM, compositing and QC in one pass, with runtime and aspect ratio explicitly confirmed
- Physical continuity rules: objects enter, contact, settle and lock logically
